How to Export / Print Perfect Scantrons

To print physical copies or export exact-dimension PDFs of your customized answer sheet, follow these instructions:

  1. Click the Print Sheet button in the top navigation bar.
  2. In your browser's Print dialog, set the destination (select either your physical printer or Save as PDF).
  3. Ensure the Layout Match matches your selected size: e.g. Letter.
  4. Under More Settings:
    • Set Margins to None or Default.
    • Toggle Background graphics to ON to render red bubbles and scanner marking elements correctly.
Tip: If you are running multiple choice sets larger than 100 questions, consider formatting into 4 columns to fit onto a single page without overflowing.
